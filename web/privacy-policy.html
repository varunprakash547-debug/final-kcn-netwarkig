Krishi Credit Network - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 August 2026

Information we collect

KCN may collect account information such as name, email, mobile number, KCN ID, referral code, location details, credit/payment records, orders, points and rewards information needed to operate the service.

Aadhaar-related information

Where Aadhaar-based identity verification is enabled, KCN uses the information only for the stated verification purpose and applicable compliance requirements. KCN does not display the full Aadhaar number in normal app screens. Appropriate consent is requested before processing.

How we use information

We use information to provide KCN services, maintain credit records, process orders and payments, provide support, prevent misuse, maintain security, comply with applicable law and improve the service.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information is shared only where necessary to provide a requested service, with authorized users according to the app's permissions, with service providers acting for KCN, or where legally required.

Security

We use access controls, authentication and other reasonable safeguards. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

Retention and deletion

Users may request account deletion through the account-deletion page. Records that must be retained for legal, fraud-prevention, accounting or credit/audit purposes may be retained for the required period.
